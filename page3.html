01 / OUR STORY

A shared frustration.
A local solution.

York-shire Airport started with a frustration we shared. Living in the Rock Hill area near Charlotte Douglas International Airport, we were tired of the long waits to secure hangar space for our planes. We were not the only people who felt this way, so we made a goal.

Our goal was to create a place that could help unburden CLT Airport and create a space for our local community.

With convenient hangar space and friendly service, we wanted to give pilots a place where they could spend less time waiting and more time flying.

04 / BEFORE YOU ARRIVE

Frequently asked
questions.

Do I need to own a plane to visit?

While we are mainly in the hangar storage business, you do not need to own a plane to visit. To create a reservation, you just need the plane’s information and your identification.

Can I visit just to watch planes take off?

We are located in a very open area. While you can visit to watch planes, you can also watch them just as well from outside the fenced area.

Can I rent a hangar for just one night?

Yes, you can rent a hangar for just one night if needed.
